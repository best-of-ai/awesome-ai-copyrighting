## General Resources

* **[OpenAI Cookbook](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook)** - Practical examples of GPT-based writing and tools.

## AI Copywriting Platforms

* **[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/)** - Versatile AI writing assistant for blog posts, emails, and ads.
* **[Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai/)** - Popular tool for generating marketing copy and product descriptions.
* **[Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/)** - AI tool for landing pages, blogs, and ad campaigns.
* **[Anyword](https://anyword.com/)** - Copywriting with performance prediction for different audiences.
* **[Hypotenuse AI](https://www.hypotenuse.ai/)** - Focused on e-commerce content and product descriptions.

## Open-Source Projects

* **[text-generation-webui](https://github.com/oobabooga/text-generation-webui)** - Run LLMs locally in a browser interface.
* **[gpt-2-simple](https://github.com/minimaxir/gpt-2-simple)** - Easy interface for fine-tuning GPT-2.
* **[LibreChat](https://github.com/danny-avila/LibreChat)** - ChatGPT-style UI for self-hosted models.
* **[FlowGPT](https://flowgpt.com/)** - Community prompt sharing platform.

## Prompt Engineering Tools

* **[PromptHero](https://prompthero.com/)** - Marketplace and search for prompts.
* **[AIPRM for ChatGPT](https://www.aiprm.com/)** - Prompt templates for SEO, copywriting, ads.
* **[FlowGPT Prompts](https://flowgpt.com/)** - Community-created prompts for AI tools.

## Marketing & Ad Copy

* **[Jasper Templates](https://www.jasper.ai/templates)** - Ad, headline, and CTA copywriting prompts.
* **[Copy.ai Marketing Templates](https://www.copy.ai/templates/)** - Templates for Facebook, Google, and social media ads.
* **[Ocoya](https://www.ocoya.com/)** - AI-powered social media scheduling and ad copy generation.

## Email & Newsletter Copy

* **[Smartwriter](https://www.smartwriter.ai/)** - Personalized cold emails and follow-ups.
* **[Lavender](https://www.lavender.ai/)** - AI email assistant for improving tone, structure, and engagement.
* **[ConvertKit + AI](https://convertkit.com/)** - Email marketing platform with AI-enhanced writing tools.

## SEO-Focused Tools

* **[Surfer AI](https://surferseo.com/ai/)** - Combines SEO and AI-generated articles.
* **[Outranking](https://www.outranking.io/)** - Guided SEO writing workflows.
* **[NeuronWriter](https://neuronwriter.com/)** - Optimized long-form AI content for SEO.
* **[Screpy AI Article Generator](https://screpy.com/feature/ai-article-generator/)** - Creates editable SEO content drafts, outlines, and briefs from topics and keyword ideas.
